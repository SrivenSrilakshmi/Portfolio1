About

I work in Network Security Operations with a strong focus on firewall engineering, secure remote access, and incident-driven troubleshooting. I design, implement, and maintain firewall policies, analyze traffic flows end-to-end, and secure network paths using packet-level analysis, log correlation, and targeted automation. In parallel, I founded and lead an independent cybersecurity lab where I collaborate with a small team to design hands-on security projects, simulate real-world network and security scenarios, and investigate practical security challenges. This work strengthens my ability to think systematically about network behavior, failure modes, and defensive controls. I have strong fundamentals in TCP/IP, routing and switching, VLANs, NAT, and dynamic routing protocols such as OSPF and BGP, which I apply directly when diagnosing complex connectivity and security issues across enterprise-style environments. I am the author of the research paper “End-to-End Privacy-Aware Federated Learning for Wearable Health Devices via Encrypted Aggregation in Programmable Networks.” The paper explores secure data aggregation using encryption and programmable network elements to preserve privacy in distributed systems. This research strengthens my understanding of secure data flows, encrypted traffic handling, and privacy-aware system design — concepts that directly inform my approach to network security and traffic analysis.
