About

I work in Network Security Operations with a strong focus on firewall engineering, secure remote access, and incident-driven troubleshooting. I design, implement, and maintain firewall policies, analyze traffic flows end-to-end, and secure network paths using packet-level analysis, log correlation, and targeted automation.

I founded and lead an independent cybersecurity lab where I work with a small team to simulate real-world network security scenarios, validate firewall and routing behavior, and build repeatable troubleshooting and hardening workflows.

I am the author of the research paper “End-to-End Privacy-Aware Federated Learning for Wearable Health Devices via Encrypted Aggregation in Programmable Networks.” This work strengthened my understanding of encrypted traffic behavior and secure data flows.
